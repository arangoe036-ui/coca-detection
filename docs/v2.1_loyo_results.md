# v2.1 — Leave-One-Year-Out (LOYO) results

Multi-year model (2019–2024 pooled), per-year input normalization, frozen
train-years AOI-total calibration. For each held-out year: train on the other
five years' TRAIN blocks, early-stop on their VAL blocks, predict the held-out
year, apply the calibration scalar fit on training years only (never the held-out
year). This is the honest "predict an unseen year at these locations" test — the
2026 use case.

## Out-of-year predicted / official ratio

| Held-out year | Ratio | Predicted ha | Official ha | val MAE | scalar |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 2019 | 0.95 | 34,631 | 36,296 | 0.0190 | 1.13 |
| 2020 | **0.59** | 20,864 | 35,277 | 0.0217 | 1.37 |
| 2021 | 0.90 | 34,458 | 38,285 | 0.0219 | 1.13 |
| 2022 | **1.48** | 56,336 | 37,965 | 0.0193 | 1.18 |
| 2023 | 1.01 | 40,175 | 39,815 | 0.0212 | 1.19 |
| 2024 | 0.79 | 34,925 | 44,240 | 0.0206 | 1.20 |

**Spread:** min 0.59 · max 1.48 · **mean 0.95** · std ±0.27

## Interpretation

- **Mean 0.95 → no systematic bias.** Averaged over years the calibration is well
  centred; the problem is *year-to-year variance*, not a consistent over/under.
- **Spread is wide (0.59–1.48, ±0.27), not the ±10–15% gate target.** Multi-year
  training + per-year normalization + frozen calibration did **not** tighten
  out-of-year magnitude to the target band.
- **Spatial fit is uniformly strong** (val MAE ~0.02 every fold) — the model learns
  *where* coca is well in every year. The instability is purely in *absolute
  hectares* for an unseen year.
- 2024 improved modestly (0.71 in v2 Part C → 0.79 here); 2020 (0.59) and 2022
  (1.48) are the widest misses — plausibly per-year imagery quality (2020 cloud
  cover; 2022 sparse Sentinel-1 after S1B failed late 2021).

## Decision-gate call

Per the v2.1 spec: the LOYO band did **not** tighten to ±10–15%, so the honest
conclusion is that **free Sentinel imagery + ~1 km official labels have a real
ceiling on tracking absolute magnitude for an unseen year** (~±40% band).

- **Trustworthy:** coca *location* and municipal *ranking* (Tibú #1 every year),
  and *within-year* calibrated totals (2023 fit = 39,815 ha).
- **Not trustworthy as a firm figure:** absolute hectares for a year with no
  official census → the LOYO spread (~±40%) is the honest error band.
- **2026 nowcast:** may only be presented as **exploratory**, with the ±40% band,
  never as a validated figure.

Conditional next step (spec Phase 5): a temporal U-TAE model *might* tighten the
band, but it requires re-exporting per-season composites for all years and a new
architecture — a large effort with uncertain payoff against this ceiling.
