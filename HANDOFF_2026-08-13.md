### The three defects that were fixed (the project's real story)

1. **S2 `BOA_ADD_OFFSET` ignored.** ESA Processing Baseline 04.00 (from 2022-01-25) requires
   `(DN − 1000)/10000`. The code used `DN/10000`, inflating 2022–2024 by ~0.1 — a fake step in
   the middle of the series. Now **−0.0023** across the boundary instead of **+0.1000**.
2. **`scl_mask_classes` omitted SCL 0 (NO_DATA) and 1 (SATURATED).** 25.3% of the 2019/2020 AOI
   was silently blank and trained on as real ground.
3. **Split assignment by top-left corner.** Straddling tiles landed in two folds at once.

**The finding worth leading with:** defects 1 and 2 pointed in *opposite* directions on
*complementary* halves of the time series (offset inflated 2022–2024; blank coverage suppressed
2019–2021). They cancelled. The published headline — "unbiased but wide, mean 0.95, std 0.271"
— was substantially two bugs neutralising each other. **The corruption produced a
publishable-looking number, which is why it survived review.**

**The registered rule (`docs/BASELINE_PREREG.md`, A16 + the A19 amendment at the end):**

- The three persistence variants are `persistence_last_year` (P1: prior year's presence mask),
  `persistence_freq_ever` (P2 at 0.02 = "present in any train year"), and
  `persistence_freq_majority` (P2 at 0.5 = "present in most train years"). **A fold's
  persistence score is the MAXIMUM of the three** — declared in advance so adding a variant can
  only raise the model's bar, never lower it.
- Metric: `src/evaluate.py:_metrics_at(pred, target, thr=0.02, t_thr=0.0)` — the identical
  function and cuts the U-Net / RF / NDVI arms already use for `presence_iou`. Labels are
  cell-constant (rasterised from the official ~1 km grid), so pixel IoU **is** area-weighted
  cell IoU. Do not introduce a second granularity; that is a second chance at the metric.
- P1 for the 2019 fold uses **2020** — a future year. Deliberate: P2 is already non-causal, and
  a non-causal P1 only strengthens the null.
- **Decision:** U-Net beats the persistence score in **≥5/6 folds** ⇒ the spatial claim is
  earned and publishable as a model result. **3–4/6** ⇒ report as indistinguishable from
  persistence; the claim shrinks to "reproduces the official spatial pattern," with persistence
  named as an equally good method. **≤2/6** ⇒ **the spatial claim is not publishable as a model
  result** — report it as a negative result with the same prominence as the counting one.
  **Do not retune the U-Net in response.**
- One asymmetry recorded in the model's disfavour: the U-Net's Track A `presence_iou` is
  computed after the A5 calibration multiplier `a` (train-blocks only, leakage-free), shifting
  its effective cut to 0.02/a. `a` is near 1. It is not being removed.

## 8. Traps that have already cost hours — do not rediscover these

- `Start-Process -PassThru` **discards the native handle**, so `$p.ExitCode` comes back **empty**
  after exit, even after `WaitForExit()`. Reading `$null = $p.Handle` while the process is alive
  caches it. Without that line, three *finished* export years were treated as failures and
  re-downloaded.
- Planetary Computer SAS tokens expire and `planetary_computer` **reuses the same token
  in-process**, so an in-process retry gets another 403 forever. Only a **new process** gets
  fresh credentials: `stac_export.py` exits **75**, `scripts/run_export.ps1` relaunches on it.
- `_is_expired_creds` must anchor its regex. A bare `"403"` match made a **404** on scene
  `S2A_MSIL2A_2019`**`0403`**`T150719` look like expired credentials. Regression test exists.
- **The product, not the exit code, decides completion.** `run_export.ps1` checks for the mosaic
  on disk at the top of every loop iteration.
- Count progress by **files on disk**, never by log lines: a resumed run reprints "reusing" for
  every finished tile, so line-counting can report >12/12.
- `--subtile-deg` **does change the output mosaic footprint** (`ov = step_deg * 0.02`). 2023 came
  out 5018×5595 at a 320 m offset and needed `scripts/align_mosaic_grid.py`.
- A guard placed after an optional import is **inert** — `rio_cogeo` is not installed, so
  `ModuleNotFoundError` fired before the 75 m check ever ran. Four separate inert checks were
  written this project. **After adding any safety check, prove it fires.**
- The `--smoke` flag used to write into the **production** metrics file and **production**
  checkpoint filenames (`loyo_fold_2023.pt`). Its 2-epoch outputs are quarantined in
  `outputs/DISCARDED/` with a README. Verify the smoke path still writes somewhere harmless.
- `encoder_weights: ssl4eo` in config is a **no-op** — it lands in `geo_keys`, passes `None` to
  smp, and `_load_geo_encoder_weights` only warns. **The encoder is randomly initialised.**
  `imagenet` is deferred as a separate pre-registered ablation, not a quiet upgrade.
- The per-year input normalisation is **transductive, not a leak**. It uses the test year's own
  *input* statistics, which any real deployment also has. Earlier project notes calling it a
  leak were wrong.
