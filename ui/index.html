    How to read this

    Totals and hotspot ranking are validated against Colombia's official ~1 km grid, so totals and locations are reliable — individual plot boundaries are not. The imagery is for visual context; the model does not resolve single plots.

    Satellite imagery is a multi-date mosaic and may not match 2023; remote areas may be lower resolution.

    The model is calibrated on one region-year (Catatumbo, 2023).

    Density: model on free Sentinel-1/2. Official: Colombia ODC / datos.gov.co.
