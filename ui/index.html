    How to read this

    Location and ranking are reliable — where coca is, and which municipalities lead (Tibú first every year), match the official record. Individual plot boundaries are not resolved; the imagery is for visual context only.

    Absolute hectares are reliable only for years with an official census. For a year without one, out-of-sample validation shows totals can be off by roughly ±40% — read the number as an estimate with that band, not a firm figure.

    Years without an official census (2025 onward) are shown as an unvalidated nowcast — UNODC figures lag ~18 months. Satellite imagery is a multi-date mosaic and may not match the analysis year.

    Density: model on free Sentinel-1/2. Official: Colombia ODC / datos.gov.co.
