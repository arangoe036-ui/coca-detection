    How to read this

    This map answers where, not how much. Hectare totals are out of scope: a pre-registered test found no imagery-based estimator beats simply averaging past official counts, because coca covers ~3.6% of this region and its year-to-year change is under 1% of the area — far below normal seasonal variation.

    Ranking is the stronger claim; per-municipality accuracy varies a lot. The largest municipalities rank correctly (Tibú leads every year), but agreement with the official census ranges from about 0.05× to 1.69× across the ten municipalities. Read the ranking, not any single municipality's number.

    Resolution: ~1 km cells, not fields. The official labels are a ~1 km grid with one value spread across every pixel of each cell, so this can only agree about which ~1 km cells contain coca. Individual plot boundaries are never resolved, and published rasters are deliberately blurred to 75 m. The satellite imagery is a multi-date mosaic for visual context and may not match the analysis year.

    Metrics are being re-measured (2026-08-10). An internal audit found two preprocessing defects and a train/test overlap in the evaluation split. Numbers previously published here were withdrawn rather than adjusted; the corrected re-run is pre-registered.

    Density: model on free Sentinel-1/2. Official: Colombia ODC / datos.gov.co.
